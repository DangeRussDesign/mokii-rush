Privacy Policy — Mokii Rush

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Mokii Rush (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is developed by One Thirteen Labs. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. By using Mokii Rush, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

Mokii Rush does not require you to create an account and does not ask you for your name, email address, or other directly identifying information. Your game progress (coins, unlocked characters, streaks, best scores, and settings) is stored only on your device.

The app uses third-party services for advertising and analytics. Through these services, the following categories of information may be collected automatically:

  • Advertising & device identifiers — including the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), where you have permitted tracking.
  • Usage and interaction data — such as app opens, screens viewed, runs played, and in-app events, used to understand and improve the game.
  • Device and technical data — such as device model, operating-system version, language, and general (coarse) region.
  • Approximate diagnostics — crash and performance information, where available.

App Tracking Transparency

On first launch, iOS may ask whether you allow the app to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you decline, the app still works fully — you will simply see less personalized (non-tailored) ads. You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Advertising

Mokii Rush shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use device and advertising identifiers to deliver and measure ads. You can learn more, and manage your ad preferences, in Google’s policies below.

Analytics

We use Google Firebase / Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used (for example, how many runs are played) so we can improve it. This data is reported in aggregate.

Third-party services

Information handled by these providers is subject to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob & Firebase — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use their services — https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Apple (App Store, device) — https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Children’s privacy

Mokii Rush is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided such information, please contact us and we will address it.

Your choices

  • Decline or revoke app tracking at any time via iOS Settings (see above).
  • Reset your advertising identifier in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising.
  • Delete the app to remove the locally-stored game data from your device.

Data retention

Game progress remains on your device until you delete the app. Data handled by our advertising and analytics providers is retained according to their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new “Last updated” date.
